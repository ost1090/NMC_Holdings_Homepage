Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This website is operated by NMC Holdings Group as a parent company information and governance hub. NMC Holdings Group itself does not trade directly with the public.

The trading activities referenced on this website are conducted by associated businesses, including AllGoods Direct, ClipNation Studio, and Digital Gold eBooks. This privacy policy explains how information is handled in connection with those businesses.

Information We Collect

This website does not actively collect personal information through forms, user accounts, or transactional systems.

Any personal information you choose to provide voluntarily — such as by contacting one of the associated trading businesses via email — is provided directly to that business and used solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or conducting legitimate business activities.

Use of Information

Personal information provided to a trading business may be used only to:

  • Respond to enquiries or communications
  • Provide products or services where applicable
  • Maintain operational, accounting, and compliance records
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations

Neither NMC Holdings Group nor its associated trading businesses sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking purposes.

Limited, anonymous analytics may be used in the future to understand general website usage and improve performance. Any such data would be aggregated and non-identifiable.

Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites operated by associated businesses or external platforms. NMC Holdings Group is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken by each trading business to protect information provided to them. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact the relevant trading business directly using the email addresses provided on this website.
