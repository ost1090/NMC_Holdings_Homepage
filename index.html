NMC Holdings Group is an Australian-owned enterprise managing a modern portfolio of digital assets. Established in 2011 as a commercial real estate holding company, we have since evolved into an AI-driven digital business group.

By treating artificial intelligence and automation as a core operational layer, we run a highly efficient, solopreneur-led model that achieves enterprise-level scalability without traditional organizational overhead.

Our mission is to build sustainable, operationally sound businesses that leverage practical AI innovations to drive disciplined growth in the modern digital economy.

A central defining pillar of our operational structure is Direct IT Group, Australia's premier on-call mobile mechanic directory. This automated SaaS platform serves as a high-impact, localized service directory, connecting tradespeople and customers efficiently while simplifying digital management for their businesses.
