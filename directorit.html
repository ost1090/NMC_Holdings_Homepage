Direct IT Group is Australia's premier SaaS-based online directory and lead-generation platform, engineered specifically for the automotive and localized trades sector.

Operating as a highly automated, high-impact service directory, the platform seamlessly connects on-call mobile mechanics and tradespeople with customers requiring immediate, location-based assistance.

Local businesses partner with Direct IT Group through predictable, tiered monthly memberships. In return, they are provided with a dedicated business listing, hyper-localized lead generation routing, and streamlined profile management tools.

By leveraging advanced workflows and scalable cloud infrastructure, Direct IT Group simplifies digital administration for tradespeople. This allows them to focus entirely on their craft and service delivery, while the platform autonomously handles their digital visibility and customer acquisition.
