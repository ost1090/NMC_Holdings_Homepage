AllGoods Direct is an Australian-focused online retail venture offering a broad range of consumer products through a modern, dropship-enabled supply model. The business is designed to provide customers with access to everyday and specialised products while maintaining clear delivery expectations and consistent service standards.

The focus of AllGoods Direct is on building a practical, scalable retail operation that prioritises operational efficiency, transparent communication, and responsible sourcing across multiple product categories.

Operating Model

AllGoods Direct partners with approved wholesale and distribution suppliers to provide access to a wide product catalogue without maintaining physical inventory. This approach allows the business to operate with reduced overheads while remaining flexible as product ranges and supplier relationships evolve over time.

Supplier selection is guided by availability, reliability, and fulfilment capability, with an emphasis on maintaining predictable delivery timeframes and clear customer communication throughout the order lifecycle.

Group Relationship

AllGoods Direct operates as a trading business within the NMC Holdings Group structure. The venture benefits from shared governance, operational oversight, and internal automation systems developed at the group level.

As part of the broader group framework, AllGoods Direct is positioned to expand its product range and platform capabilities in line with long-term viability and sustainable growth objectives.
